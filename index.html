Scotland is a beautiful country that is apart of the United Kingdom. It offers scenery ranging from mountains, rivers, forrests, and to fields. It snows in the winter and has comfortable temperatures in the summer. Scotland also offers a variety of living like busy cities and rural towns. Scotland also has a very interesting culture and deep rooted traditions.
